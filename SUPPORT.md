# Support

## Self-service

- **Documentation**: [regira.github.io/Regira-Packages](https://regira.github.io/Regira-Packages/) — per-package guides, examples, and a [quickstart](docs/quickstart.md)
- **MCP server**: connect `https://mcp.regira.com/mcp` and let your AI agent search the docs, fetch setup guides, and scaffold code — see the [README](README.md#connect-the-mcp-server-recommended)
- **Samples**: [Regira-Samples](https://github.com/Regira/Regira-Samples) and the runnable [`tests/Entities.TestApi`](tests/Entities.TestApi)

## Questions and bugs

- Open a [GitHub issue](https://github.com/Regira/Regira-Packages/issues) — also fine for usage questions.

## Commercial support

License holders and prospective customers can reach Regira at [b2b@regira.com](mailto:b2b@regira.com) or via the [contact form](https://regira.com/contact). Licensing details: [licensing.md](licensing.md).
